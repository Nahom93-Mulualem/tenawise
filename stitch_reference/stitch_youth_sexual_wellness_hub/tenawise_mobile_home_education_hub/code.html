Can an STI have no symptoms?
auto_awesome TenaWise AI Assistant

Yes. Some sexually transmitted infections may cause few or no noticeable symptoms. Testing can be important because symptoms alone cannot reliably determine whether someone has an STI.

* TenaWise provides educational information and does not replace professional medical care.
