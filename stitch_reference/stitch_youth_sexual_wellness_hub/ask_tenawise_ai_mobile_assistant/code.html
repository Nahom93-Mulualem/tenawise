TenaWise Companion Verified Advice

Yes, absolutely. Many sexually transmitted infections—such as Chlamydia, Gonorrhea, HPV, and early-stage HIV—can cause few or completely unnoticeable symptoms, especially during initial weeks or months. This is very common, so you are not alone in wondering about this.

fact_check Key things to remember:
  • check_circle Asymptomatic transmission: STIs can still be transmitted to partners even when no symptoms exist.
  • check_circle Routine screening: A simple lab screening test is the only reliable way to know for sure.
  • check_circle Accessible in Ethiopia: Free, confidential rapid screening is offered at youth centers like Marie Stopes Ethiopia and FGAE clinics nationwide.
