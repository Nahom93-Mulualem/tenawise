## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an empathetic, privacy-first health-tech platform for Ethiopian youth navigating sexual and reproductive health. The interface actively departs from cold, sterile clinical software, embracing instead an organic, grounded, and welcoming aesthetic rooted in psychological safety and cultural dignity.

### Brand Personality & Core Pillars
- **Radical Discretion & Privacy:** Visual reassurance that the user is secure, anonymous, and protected from prying eyes at all times.
- **Warmth & De-stigmatization:** Soft botanical tones, tactile surfaces, and respectful vernacular that remove shame, judgment, and clinical coldness.
- **Cultural Resonance & Accessibility:** Purposefully designed for dual-script harmony (Latin and Ge'ez/Ethiopic scripts), acknowledging local context without resorting to ornamental clichés.
- **Clarity & Agency:** Straightforward, non-condescending health guidance paired with swift, frictionless navigation and fail-safe safety features.

### Visual Style
The design style integrates **Warm Organic Modernism** with modern startup utility: generous cream negative space, soft earthen boundaries, gentle surface layering, and rounded geometry that feels approachable and human.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from native Ethiopian flora, highland landscapes, and warm earthenware. It establishes a comforting, grounded sanctuary while ensuring strong WCAG AAA/AA readability across mobile screens under varying lighting conditions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary — Botanical Sage Green (`#2D5A43`):** Conveys growth, life, balance, and health without medical detachment. Used for key interactive states, active headers, and reassuring confirmations.
- **Deep Forest Green (`#1B3B2B`):** Deep foundation color for high-emphasis containers, footer surfaces, and anchoring elements.
- **Soft Sage (`#E2ECE6`):** Gentle wash for active card outlines, badge backgrounds, and secondary focus states.
- **Secondary — Muted Blue-Green (`#4A7C72`):** Calm companion hue applied to informational tags, subtle secondary actions, and iconography.
- **Tertiary / Accent — Warm Terracotta (`#C86446`):** Inspired by Ethiopian clay and earthen architecture. Deployed selectively for vital callouts, interactive accents, confidential badge highlights, and youth engagement prompts.
- **Neutral / Text — Charcoal Forest (`#222B27`):** Deep, organic charcoal optimized for high legibility without the jarring starkness of pure black (`#000000`).
- **Surface Canvas — Soft Cream (`#FBF9F5`):** Warm, inviting base canvas that softens screen glare and eliminates sterile hospital white.
- **Card Surfaces — Pure Off-White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity resting surface for content cards, interactive inputs, and conversation bubbles.
- **Discreet Emergency Red (`#A83232`):** Specially tuned deep red dedicated solely to the persistent "Quick Exit" panic affordance.

## Typography

The typographic system provides natural, unstrained bilingual reading across Latin and Ethiopic (Amharic, Tigrinya, Oromo via Latin/Fidäl) script environments.

### Dual-Script Pairing Strategy
- **Headlines & Display:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides a welcoming, modern, humanized geometry. For Amharic and Ge'ez display headlines, fall back seamlessly to `Noto Sans Ethiopic` bold weights.
- **Body & Longform Guidance:** `Noto Sans` paired alongside `Noto Sans Ethiopic`. Ethiopic script glyphs require balanced line-heights due to vertical proportions and diacritic markers; standard line-heights are intentionally set to `1.5` to `1.6` across body tiers to prevent any character crowding.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium/semi-bold weights deliver immediate clarity for navigational chips, privacy warnings, and time indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid grid anchors the system, engineered to run smoothly on diverse mobile hardware while expanding into a calm, centered reading sanctuary on wider desktop viewports.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Compact (Mobile: < 640px):** 4-column fluid layout, `1rem` margin, `1rem` gutter. Content stretches to full container boundaries while preserving thumb-friendly safe margins.
- **Medium (Tablet: 640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margin, `1.5rem` gutter. Max reading column width capped to maintain intimacy.
- **Expanded (Desktop: > 1024px):** 12-column layout, max-width bounded to `1200px` centered, `3rem` outer margin, `2rem` gutter. Health education articles constrain strictly to a 680px center spine for optimal cognitive intake.

### Spatial Rhythm
All spacing maps strictly to an 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`) with a 4px fine-adjustment token (`0.25rem`) used for compact badge and tag interiors. Generous vertical breathing room between sensitive topic sections prevents cognitive overload and anxiety.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **organic tonal surfaces and diffused ambient lighting**, rejecting aggressive, hard drop shadows or synthetic plastic gloss.

### Elevation Levels
- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Unraised `#FBF9F5` Soft Cream background.
- **Resting Surface (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` card surfaces sitting on the cream plane, framed by a soft hairline border (`1px solid #E2ECE6`) and a quiet ambient floor shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.05)`.
- **Floating & Interactive (Level 2):** Hovered modules, dropdown menus, and confidential filter trays: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.08)`.
- **Persistent Protective Overlays (Level 3):** Modal dialogs, bottom sheets, and the Quick Exit bar: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(34, 43, 39, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a friendly, protective shape language. Sharp right angles convey clinical rigidity, while excessive pill shapes across entire layouts feel overly juvenile. Level 2 roundedness strikes the ideal balance of modern startup maturity and youthful softness.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Micro UI (Badges, Checkboxes):** `0.375rem` (6px) rounded corners.
- **Interactive Inputs & Primary Buttons:** `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) corners.
- **Content Cards & Panels:** `1rem` (16px) standard; featured storytelling cards use `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Pills (Status Tags, Quick Action Pills):** Fully circular pills (`9999px`) reserved strictly for filtering chips and interactive category pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Botanical Sage (`#2D5A43`) with White (`#FFFFFF`) text, subtle 12px rounded corners, 48px touch target height. Active hover darkens to Forest Green (`#1B3B2B`).
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined Soft Sage (`1.5px solid #2D5A43`) on transparent or cream background with Botanical Sage typography.
- **Accent Action:** Warm Terracotta (`#C86446`) fill for urgent peer-to-peer advice lines or self-care check-ins.

### Quick Exit Affordance (Safety Component)
A high-visibility, always-accessible button anchored in the top navigation or sticky mobile bar:
- Styled with an understated dark graphite or muted emergency tint (`#A83232`), labeled with universal quick-read text ("Quick Exit / ፈጣን መውጫ") alongside an escape icon.
- Triggers instant tab redirect to an innocuous educational or weather portal and wipes current session route state.

### Confidentiality & Safety Badges
- Encapsulated pills featuring an understated lock or shield glyph paired with reassuring microcopy (e.g., *"100% Anonymous" / "ሚስጥራዊ"*).
- Background: Soft Sage (`#E2ECE6`) with Deep Forest Green (`#1B3B2B`) text, or Warm Terracotta light tint (`rgba(200, 100, 70, 0.1)`) with Terracotta text.

### Cards
- Resting on `#FFFFFF` with `16px` border radii and a subtle `#E2ECE6` border.
- Generous internal padding (`1.5rem` on desktop, `1rem` on mobile) ensures sensitive question-and-answer content never feels cramped.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) enclosed in a `1.5px` border of `#E2ECE6`.
- Focus state: Smooth transition to Botanical Sage (`#2D5A43`) ring with an ambient `rgba(45, 90, 67, 0.15)` glow.
- Helper labels provide transparent, non-judgmental context explaining why a preference (such as age group or region) is requested and confirming that no real identity is ever logged.

### Topic Chips & Filters
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` background, `#222B27` charcoal text, `#E2ECE6` border.
- Active: Botanical Sage (`#2D5A43`) background, `#FFFFFF` text. Pill-shaped (`9999px`) for quick thumb-scrolling across youth reproductive health categories.
