# Ethiopian Ministry of Health & WHO Knowledge Documents

Place your official PDF guidelines and manuals in this folder:

- `ethiopia_youth_srh_guidelines.pdf` — National Adolescent and Youth Health Strategy
- `national_pep_guidelines.pdf` — Guidelines for Comprehensive HIV Prevention / PEP & PrEP
- `family_planning_manual.pdf` — National Family Planning Guideline & Contraceptive Protocols
- Any additional WHO or MoH SRH documents

When you run `python rag_engine/ingest.py`, all documents in this directory will be automatically parsed, chunked, and embedded into the local vector database.
